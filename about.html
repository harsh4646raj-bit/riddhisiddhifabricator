About Our Workshop

Architectural Fabrication Built on Precision & Trust

Riddhi Siddhi Fabricator is an established architectural fabrication workshop located at New Police Line, Muzaffarpur, Bihar. With over a decade of hands-on fabrication experience, we engineer custom uPVC, aluminium, and steel solutions for residential and commercial spaces across Muzaffarpur and neighbouring North Bihar districts.

10+ Years of Direct Workshop Craftsmanship

Founded with a commitment to architectural durability and exact on-site fitment, Riddhi Siddhi Fabricator operates directly from our workshop at New Police Line, Muzaffarpur. We do not act as middlemen or third-party brokers. Every window system, entrance door, structural gate, and decorative railing is measured, fabricated, and installed by our own skilled team.

We specialize in tailored architectural requirements for residential homes, apartment complexes, commercial offices, retail storefronts, and institutional buildings across Muzaffarpur, Vaishali, Samastipur, Darbhanga, Sitamarhi, Motihari, and nearby areas.

Our Core Fabrication Specialties

Our fabrication capabilities span three core architectural materials, each selected and engineered for specific environmental and structural requirements:

Material Priority 1

uPVC Windows & Doors

Multi-chamber uPVC sliding windows, casement windows, and uPVC bathroom doors. Designed for dust protection, weather sealing, and low maintenance without rust or rot.

Material Priority 2

Architectural Aluminium Glazing

Slimline sliding windows, arched windows, heavy casement doors, office glass partitions, ACP cladding, and display enclosures. Fabricated with precision mitre joints, anodized finishes, and weather-sealed glass channels.

Material Priority 3

Structural Steel & Gates

Heavy-duty main entrance gates, precision CNC laser-cut designer gates, residential safety grill enclosures, and custom staircase railing systems built with structural welded construction for outdoor durability.
