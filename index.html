About Us

Built on Trust

Riddhi Siddhi Fabricator is a premier fabrication workshop located at New Police Line, near Gausia Masjid, Muzaffarpur, Bihar. With over a decade of dedicated craftsmanship, we specialize in custom aluminium, uPVC, and stainless steel solutions for residential and commercial spaces across Muzaffarpur and neighbouring Bihar districts. Every installation is built with verified material grades, welded construction, and expert on-site fitting.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About Our Fabrication Work

Direct, transparent answers regarding our materials, custom fabrication process, and quotation estimates.

What architectural fabrication services does Riddhi Siddhi Fabricator provide?

Riddhi Siddhi Fabricator specializes in custom aluminium sliding windows, casement doors, office glass partitions, uPVC windows, uPVC bathroom doors, custom stainless steel main entrance gates, CNC laser-cut designer gates, and crystal acrylic staircase railings.

Where is the fabrication workshop located and which areas do you serve?

Our physical workshop is located at New Police Line, near Gausia Masjid, Muzaffarpur, Bihar 842001, India. We provide custom fabrication, on-site measurement, and professional installation across Muzaffarpur, Vaishali, Samastipur, Darbhanga, Sitamarhi, Motihari, and neighbouring districts of North Bihar.

What materials are used for steel gates and railings?

We use high-grade architectural stainless steel with welded construction to ensure structural strength, rust resistance, and durability in outdoor weather.

What are the benefits of uPVC windows over traditional frames?

Our uPVC windows feature multi-chamber profiles with weather-sealed construction, airtight dust seals, and low maintenance without rusting.

How can I request a price estimate or fabrication quotation?

You can request a free quotation online through our Quote Form or by contacting us directly via WhatsApp or Call at +91 9771086644. Pricing is calculated accurately based on your project dimensions, material grade, and on-site fitting requirements.
