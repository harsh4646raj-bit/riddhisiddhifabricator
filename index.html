About Us

Built on Trust

Riddhi Siddhi Fabricator is a premier fabrication workshop located at New Police Line, Muzaffarpur, Bihar. With over a decade of dedicated craftsmanship, we specialize in custom aluminium, uPVC, and stainless steel solutions for residential and commercial spaces across Muzaffarpur and neighbouring Bihar districts. Every installation is built with verified material grades, precision TIG welding, and expert on-site fitting.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About Our Fabrication Work

Direct, transparent answers regarding our materials, custom fabrication process, and quotation estimates.

What architectural fabrication services does Riddhi Siddhi Fabricator provide?

Riddhi Siddhi Fabricator specializes in custom aluminium sliding windows, casement doors, office glass partitions, energy-efficient uPVC windows, waterproof bathroom doors, heavy-duty Jindal 304 stainless steel main entrance gates, CNC laser-cut designer gates, and crystal acrylic staircase railings.

Where is the fabrication workshop located and which areas do you serve?

Our physical workshop is located at New Police Line, Muzaffarpur, Bihar 842001. We provide custom fabrication, on-site measurement, and professional installation across Muzaffarpur, Vaishali, Samastipur, Darbhanga, Sitamarhi, Motihari, and neighbouring districts of North Bihar.

What material grades are used for steel gates and railings?

We use authentic Jindal 304 grade stainless steel with precision TIG welding to ensure high strength, rust resistance, and decades of structural durability in outdoor weather.

What are the benefits of uPVC windows over traditional frames?

Our uPVC windows feature multi-chamber insulated profiles that provide superior acoustic sound dampening against street noise, thermal temperature control, airtight dust seals, and zero maintenance without rusting.

How can I request a price estimate or fabrication quotation?

You can request a free quotation online through our Quote Form or by contacting us directly via WhatsApp or Call at +91 9771086644. Pricing is calculated accurately based on your project dimensions, material grade, and on-site fitting requirements.
